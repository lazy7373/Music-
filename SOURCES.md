# Free music discovery sources

These are starting points for finding tracks, not blanket permission to copy every recording.

- Free Music Archive: https://freemusicarchive.org/
- Internet Archive Audio: https://archive.org/details/audio
- Jamendo: https://www.jamendo.com/
- Bandcamp: https://bandcamp.com/
- SoundCloud: https://soundcloud.com/

For each track, verify the individual licence and whether MP3 redistribution/re-upload is allowed. Record the exact licence and required attribution. Free streaming, free download, and royalty-free do not automatically mean redistribution is permitted.
