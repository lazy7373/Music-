# Free music discovery sources

These are discovery tools, not blanket permission to copy every recording.

## Sources
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) — open-licensed independent music. Check the licence on each track.
- [FreeCrate](https://freecrate.org/) — indexes downloadable tracks and displays licence categories; follow through to the original artist/source page and verify the exact licence.
- [Internet Archive Audio](https://archive.org/details/audio) — item-level rights vary; some collections are noncommercial or have additional restrictions.
- [Jamendo](https://www.jamendo.com/) — check the specific track's licence and download terms.
- [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/) — use artist-authorized downloads and confirm redistribution permission.
- [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/) — a download button alone does not grant redistribution rights.

## Required checks before adding an MP3
1. Confirm the track was released between 2025-01-01 and 2026-12-31.
2. Identify the artist/rightsholder and the original track page.
3. Verify that the exact licence or written permission explicitly allows redistribution/re-upload.
4. Save the exact licence name and URL, required attribution, and source page in `catalog/tracks.json`.
5. Do not upload tracks with unclear rights, personal-use-only terms, or restrictions incompatible with redistribution.

Free streaming, a free download, or a "royalty-free" label does not automatically mean an MP3 can be mirrored in this repository.
